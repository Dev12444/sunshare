2. Prosumers list surplus; marketplace supply climbs
3. Consumers bid; demand changes
4. The pricing engine clears the slot — show the price landing inside the corridor
5. The matching engine picks the nearest viable seller
6. **A congested feeder makes one route unattractive; the engine re-routes**
7. The trade settles; the tx hash appears on the ledger page
8. Carbon and community tiles update
9. **Closing move:** the installed PWA on a phone receives a live push
   notification — "Your agent sold 3 kWh at ₹5.20"
